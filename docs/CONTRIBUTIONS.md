# Research direction and AI assistance

LatentAtlas initiated the project, set its research direction and made the central scope decisions. Those decisions include testing whether high scores reflect an easy task, requiring fair comparisons across difficulty levels, keeping technical losses distinct from scientific failures, and separating successful control from demonstrated mechanism learning.

Codex contributed substantially to literature review, proposed designs, software, local tests, experiment operation, calculations and written analysis. Experimental participants are separate model calls operating under the task contract. The assistant helping to build and inspect the study has a different information role.

Earlier process annotations were authored with knowledge of the outcomes and do not constitute independent human assessment. This edition reports aggregate outcomes and design choices without presenting those annotations as an independent validation.

The work demonstrates research decisions and artifacts produced with AI assistance. It does not claim that every program was written unaided or that the resulting codebase measures the author's independent programming ability.
