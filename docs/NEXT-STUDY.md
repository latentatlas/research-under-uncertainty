# Proposed next study — not completed results

The proposed benchmark asks whether a source-linked process assessment predicts an agent's next failure or selects a useful intervention better than task scores and strong reference methods with matched information and resources.

The first use case is an evaluator deciding whether an agent should continue with the evidence already available, collect an additional measurement, or receive human review. False alarms, unnecessary intervention and additional cost count against the method.

The design will separate information available to the agent, information visible to the process reader and the evaluator's hidden ground truth. Assessments must precede the outcomes being predicted. Healthy examples, unavailable evidence and cases without a genuine behavioral opportunity remain distinct.

Planned comparisons include a simple process reader, a clearly documented adaptation of a relevant existing method, programmatic outcome/consistency references and the proposed evidence-linked reader. Intervention comparisons will use matched source states and resource budgets. Development and held-out mechanism families will be separated. These are design commitments, not positive results or a finalized sample-size justification.

Early external review will examine measurement validity and a concrete potential evaluation decision. A working example can later be offered to a separate team to test installation, interpretation and decision usefulness. No external user, reviewer, partnership or adoption is currently confirmed.

This public description excludes the new benchmark's hidden mechanisms, reconstructive test inputs and answer keys. Publishing the current work sample does not make the proposed next benchmark validated or prove novelty over [BoxingGym](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01540), [Corral](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18805) or [DiscoverPhysics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26087).
