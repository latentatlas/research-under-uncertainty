# Inspect a claim, follow its source, run its check

This work asks what agreement between a model's decision record and its action establishes. The submitted study develops three checks: test whether candidate rules can diverge, measure task labels separately from action correspondence, and assess record-assisted prediction on later decisions.

The A2 example explains agreement through one plan's dominance on all five criteria; A1 distinguishes selectors. The follow-up retains all 48 assigned trials and two technical losses. Its 16 B records match model proposals, with 14 single-row tables. In the prospective pilot, record/no-record correctness is 6/8 versus 7/8 for Astra and 3/8 versus 3/8 for Sol, with four purposeful histories per profile. The original 15 targets and separate final target are visible in the evidence.

Read the [submitted paper](../submission/paper/manuscript.pdf), inspect the [claim map](../browse/paper-package/CLAIM-MAP.json) and [scalar interpretation checks](../browse/paper-package/artifact/interpretation_checks.py), then follow the [reproduction guide](REPRODUCIBILITY.md). The full supplement restores both components and verifies 13 existing-evidence checks without a model service.

The study's contribution is an actionable measurement discipline supported by these cases. Record agreement, selector identification, choice prediction and full task success are distinct outcomes. The [next-study description](NEXT-STUDY.md) is an earlier proposal, not additional completed evidence.
